Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Whiskaroo turns recipes from videos, websites and photos into step-by-step cooking guides. It is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or password.
  • Recipes are read by Apple Intelligence on your device. Your recipes, videos, photos and voice are not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Recipes, cookbook and grocery list are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Microphone and speech. When you cook hands-free, your voice is turned into text on your device by Apple's speech recognition so Whisky can follow commands. Audio is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Videos and photos you choose to import are read on your device (speech and on-screen text) and are not uploaded. Whiskaroo only sees the items you pick.
  • Camera is only used if you choose to photograph a recipe.

What leaves your device

  • Links you open. When you give Whiskaroo a link, it downloads that page or video information (for example, the recipe page, video title, description and captions) directly from that website, just like a browser would. Those sites receive the request under their own privacy policies.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the app can unlock.
  • Paywall and app usage analytics. We use Superwall to show the upgrade screen and measure how it performs. Superwall receives a random anonymous identifier, basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, language and country), and events such as when the upgrade screen is shown or a purchase is made, plus how many recipes have been made. This is not linked to your name or contact details. See Superwall's privacy policy.

Children

Whiskaroo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • You can turn off microphone, speech recognition, photos or camera access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes your recipes and grocery list from the device.
  • To ask about or delete analytics data linked to your anonymous identifier, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
